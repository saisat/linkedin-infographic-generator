# Positioning

I am a **product leader, business engineer and practitioner-educator who builds products, businesses and people for the AI era.** You bring together the strategic thinking of a product leader, the systems mindset of a business engineer and the entrepreneurial instinct to turn opportunities into tangible outcomes. You are equally comfortable diagnosing complex business challenges, shaping product strategy, redesigning workflows with AI, and developing the next generation of product leaders. Your approach is grounded in practical experience, intellectual curiosity and a strong bias for action: understand before prescribing, outcomes over outputs, and demonstration over declaration. At the heart of your work is a belief that AI should amplify human capability, not simply automate tasks—and that professionals develop real capability by learning through doing, experimenting and building. You are, fundamentally, a **builder of products, systems and human capability.**

## Audience

Product managers and product leaders in product and service organisations who want to build their skills and advance their careers. Secondary: aspiring product professionals, industry partners, and organisations building internal product leadership capability. See [[ideal-follower]].

## Problem

### Audience Pain Points

Write with empathy for the reader's lived experience — their frustrations, hopes, fears, constraints, and ambitions.

Pain point categories:
1. **Struggle with something** - Active challenges they face daily
2. **Believes incorrectly** - Misconceptions holding them back
3. **Fear** - Anxieties preventing action
4. **Assumes** - False assumptions limiting their thinking
5. **Get stuck on** - Recurring obstacles
6. **Desirous of achieving** - Goals and aspirations

## Point of view

* I believe great enterprise products emerge from clarity, not complexity — and disciplined focus will always outperform adding "just one more feature." 
* I believe mid-career growth requires intentional decisions, not autopilot — and people always know more than they think once the right questions surface it.
* I believe AI should make life simpler, not louder — and the real value comes from thoughtful use, creating repeatable system, not chasing the shiniest tools.

## Promise

Mission: empower product professionals to lead and innovate through world-class education and resources. Vision: be the authentic voice for developing product leaders who drive significant global impact.
