## Vocabulary and rhythm

Stated in the brand guide (not yet checked against Sai's own posts):
- Tone: professional, approachable, dependable. On social: conversational and engaging, while staying professional.
- Language: formal yet accessible, with product-management terminology the audience uses.
- Preferred words: innovation, leadership, strategy, product management, professional development.
- Avoid: overly casual language, slang, or jargon that confuses.
- Structure: clear headings and bullet points.
